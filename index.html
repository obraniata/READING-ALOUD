How to use this tool

The passage is shown exactly as it should be read. As the student reads aloud, click any word where you notice a reading issue, then choose the type of issue from the menu. Listen as the student reads aloud and click a word the moment you notice something (a mispronunciation, a skipped or added word, a repeat, a missed pause, wrong stress, or a hesitation), then pick the matching category. Tagging is instant, so you can keep pace with a fast reader. Press Start Reading (bottom-right) when the student begins so reading speed (WCPM) can be calculated. Add overall observations in Session Notes whenever convenient. When the student finishes, press Finish & Save, then complete the optional rubrics in the result window.

Listen especially for these words and sounds

L = tongue tip touches the ridge behind the upper teeth; R = tongue curls back and touches nothing. W = rounded lips, no teeth; V = upper teeth touch the lower lip. Final S/ES must be heard (rivers, canals, cities). -ED is said /t/ after unvoiced sounds (worked), /d/ after voiced sounds (ruled), and /id/ after T or D (created). Word lists are found automatically from spelling, so a few may be off. Tag these under Mispronunciation or Intonation/Stress whenever you catch one.
